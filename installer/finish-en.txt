mpv+ 1.0.1 is installed.

1. Open "Configure TorrServer" in the Start menu if you use Lampa.
2. In Lampa, select mpv.exe from the installation folder as the external player.
3. Drag local videos into the mpv+ window to play them.

Updates: "Update mpv+" in the Start menu.
Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > mpv+.
Your personal settings remain in the installation folder after uninstalling.
