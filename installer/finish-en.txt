mpv+ is installed.

1. In the player, open "Maintenance → Configure TorrServer" if you use Lampa.
   Save the server address and credentials, then restart the player.
2. In Lampa Desktop, select mpv.exe next to mpv+.exe in the installation folder.
3. Drag local videos into the mpv+ window to play them.

Updates: "Maintenance → Component updates" in the player menu.
Uninstall: Windows Settings > Apps > mpv+.
Your personal settings are preserved after uninstalling. Extension settings
may be stored in portable_config or %APPDATA%\mpv-plus\settings.
